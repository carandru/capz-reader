# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial public development release.

- NAS/direct-folder PDF scanning.
- Filename-first titles.
- Novel, Manga, and Dou categories.
- Series grouping and standalone items.
- Natural volume sorting.
- iPad/touch-first selection UX plus desktop range selection.
- Bulk category, series, tag, read-state, favorite, archive, preview, and library-removal actions.
- Cached covers and previews.
- Reading progress and multiple reader modes, including RTL.
- Single-admin authentication.
- Read-only source-library defaults.
- Generic Docker configuration with PUID/PGID support and no machine-specific paths.
